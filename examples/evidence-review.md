# Artifact review template

This is an unreviewed template. Replace placeholders with observed evidence before recording a result. Example installation commands use fictional Atlas data; no real user or organization is assessed here.

## Identity and scope

- Status: unreviewed; no readiness, approval or external action established.
- Owner / reviewer / review date: not recorded.
- Intended artifact and permitted use: not recorded.
- Original input locations, versions, capture times and SHA-256 hashes: not collected.
- Candidate artifact path and raw SHA-256: not collected.
- Extracted text path/hash, if relevant: not collected.
- Applicable requirements/rubric version and any user-selected threshold: not recorded.

## Claim and requirement mapping

| Claim or requirement | Exact artifact quote | Supporting source excerpt and version/location | Scope, uncertainty and judgment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not yet reviewed | Not yet recorded | Not yet verified | Unreviewed |

Read the entire requirement source. Include difficult requirements, alternatives, contextual qualifiers and contradictory evidence. Separate missing evidence from evidence of absence. Do not infer production experience, personal facts or outcomes from a keyword or dependency list.

## Check receipts

| Check | Exact command/tool and input hashes | Exit/status, logs and output/report hash | Interpretation and limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Not performed | Not recorded | No result | No pass inferred |

Keep semantic/source review, automated validation, visual/text inspection and eligibility/permission decisions distinct. When testing with synthetic data, label all inputs and reported scores as synthetic. Mechanical quote matching does not establish truth or coverage. Scores from different rubrics are not interchangeable.

## Handoff

- Completed review and remaining gaps: unreviewed.
- Readiness decision and supporting reasons: not made.
- External action and positive receipt: none established.
- Current owner and next authorized action: not assigned.
- Revalidation: compare source versions and candidate artifact hash before reuse; changed bytes require renewed review. Retain original inputs and previous attempts.
